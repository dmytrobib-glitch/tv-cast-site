iPhone app

TV Cast

Put your iPhone on the big screen. Mirror your screen, play photos and videos from your library, cast videos from the web and watch your own IPTV playlists on a smart TV connected to the same Wi-Fi network.

Download on the App Store

Screen mirroring

Show everything on your iPhone screen on your TV, with sound.

Photos and videos

Play pictures and clips from your library straight to the TV.

Web video

Open a site in the built-in browser and send its video to the TV.

IPTV

Add your own M3U playlists and watch the channels on the big screen.

How we handle your data

TV Cast has no sign-up. What you mirror or cast travels over your own Wi-Fi network from your iPhone to your TV; it is not uploaded to our servers. Your IPTV playlists and settings stay on your device.

The Privacy Policy explains what the app collects, why, how long we keep it and how to exercise your rights.
