Help us help you

If something is not working, please include:

  • your iPhone model and iOS version;
  • the app version;
  • the brand and model of your TV, or the streaming device connected to it;
  • what you tapped and what happened;
  • a screenshot, if you have one.

Most connection problems come from the network. Make sure your iPhone and TV are on the same Wi-Fi network and that TV Cast is allowed to find devices on your local network in iPhone Settings, under Privacy and Security, then Local Network.

Please do not send payment details. We never need your card number or your Apple Account password.

Subscriptions and refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple, not by us. To cancel a subscription, open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. To request a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue refunds ourselves.
